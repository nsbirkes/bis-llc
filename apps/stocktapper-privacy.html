Privacy Policy — Stock Tapper

Effective Date: June 22, 2026

Birkes Innovative Solutions LLC ("BIS LLC," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Stock Tapper application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Stock Tapper.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app, including features used, gameplay activity, and session duration.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Advertising Data: If ads are displayed, ad network partners may collect device identifiers and interaction data to serve relevant ads.
  • In-App Purchase Data: Transaction records for in-app purchases, processed through the Google Play Store.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve Stock Tapper's gameplay and functionality.
  • Save and sync your game progress.
  • Analyze usage trends to enhance game balance and user experience.
  • Display advertisements, where applicable.
  • Communicate with you regarding updates, support, or changes to the service.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Ad Networks: With advertising partners to display relevant ads within the app. These partners may collect device identifiers and usage data subject to their own privacy policies.
  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party services that help us operate the app (e.g., analytics, crash reporting), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Advertising

Stock Tapper may display ads provided by third-party ad networks. These networks may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

Stock Tapper is intended for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us at info@bis-llc.com and we will promptly delete it. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other applicable regulations regarding children's data.

8. Third-Party Links

Stock Tapper may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any information.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict the processing of your data.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising.
  • Request a portable copy of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@bis-llc.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of Stock Tapper after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
